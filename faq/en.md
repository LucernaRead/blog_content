## What is Lucerna?

Lucerna is a multilingual ebook reader for learning languages through real books. Tap a word for its definition, pronunciation, and conjugation; select a sentence for a grammatical breakdown and translation; save words to a review deck — all without leaving the page.

## Is Lucerna free?

Yes. You can try it without an account (1 book, 5 saved words), and a free account raises those limits (5 books, 200 saved words) and syncs across devices. AI translation and sentence analysis run on your own LLM API key (OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google) — their free tiers are usually enough for reading.

## Which languages can I read in?

The reader works with 11 languages: English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Russian, Japanese, Korean, and Chinese. AI translation and sentence analysis currently cover English, German, and French, with more on the way.

## Can I read my own books?

Yes — upload EPUB, PDF, or TXT files. Lucerna paginates them, builds a table of contents, and indexes the full text for instant search, so your own books get the same lookup and analysis as the built-in library.

## Do I need an account?

No. Try Mode works without one. When you want to keep more books and vocabulary — and sync them with the desktop app — a free account takes a minute to create.

## How is Lucerna different from Kindle plus a translator?

You never leave the sentence you are reading. Looking up a word or translating a passage happens in place, and every word you save lands in a review deck — instead of switching apps and losing the thread.

## Is there a desktop app?

Yes — macOS, Windows, and Linux, with the same account and library. The desktop app adds offline reading, faster text-to-speech, and OS-native shortcuts.
